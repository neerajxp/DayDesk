DayDesk

A local-first archive and reader for your Yahoo mail. Your mail is stored on this machine and nowhere else.

1. Start the helper

Chrome extensions can't speak IMAP — there is no raw socket API — so DayDesk runs a small process on your machine that does. From the project root: 

npm start --workspace=helper

2. Pair the panel

The helper creates a pairing token on first run. Any website you visit can send requests to a loopback port, so this token is what keeps your mail private to DayDesk: 

cat ~/.daydesk/token

Click the DayDesk toolbar icon to open the side panel, then paste the token.

3. Add your Yahoo account

Yahoo has no public mail API, so DayDesk reads over IMAP using a third-party app password — not your real account password. The helper verifies it and hands it to your macOS Keychain.

What DayDesk archives. Yahoo's IMAP only serves the most recent 10,000 messages per folder, and DayDesk pulls the newest 1,000 of your Inbox and Sent mail on first sync.

Everything from that point forward is kept permanently, even after it rolls out of Yahoo's window. Nothing older than your first sync is backfilled.

Read-only, by design. DayDesk opens your mailboxes with IMAP EXAMINE rather than SELECT, so reading a message here never marks it read on Yahoo. There is no code path in DayDesk that writes to your mailbox.

Click the DayDesk icon in your toolbar to begin.
